About

I'm a Master's student in Computational Social Science at Linköping University, Sweden, with a background in Philosophy, Politics and Society from Radboud University in the Netherlands.

My work blends rigorous statistical methods with social theory — applying tools like Random Forest, conditional logit models, and agent-based simulations to real questions about how people form relationships and navigate social structures.

Outside of research, I serve as President of LinCSS (the Association for Computational Social Science at LiU), where I lead governance, strategy, and community-building for our student body.
